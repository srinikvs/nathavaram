Only VoterId and FirstName are required; other columns may be left out. Gender is optional: Male, Female, Other or blank, and a file without a Gender column leaves gender blank. Local/Non-Local is Local or Non-Local; a blank cell, or a file with no Local/Non-Local column, is imported as Local, while an explicit Non-Local stays Non-Local. VoterId is the unique key: a VoterId that already exists is skipped. Older files still work: "SurName", "Address", "C_City", "B.S.No", "2024 B.S.No", "Ward S No", "Local", "2024 Booth" and "2026 Ward" headers are accepted, and the removed Permanent address and Profession columns are ignored. Only .csv and .xlsx files are accepted (not legacy .xls), up to 5 MB.
